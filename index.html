our story

It started with a donut and a dream.

Before there were shops, there was a trestle table, a gazebo, and a food trailer rolling across the UK and Europe. Tom — founder of doh'hut — took a road trip down the west coast of the USA, fell in love with food truck culture, and came home with one question: what's more British than a raspberry jam donut?

From his parents' kitchen to the streets of Leeds, doh'hut grew into something nobody expected. In 2018 they won the British Street Food Awards — beating the best in the country and being crowned champions by culinary legends Richard Corrigan and Gizzi Erskine. The following weekend they were in Berlin, walking away with Best Dessert at the European Street Food Awards. Not bad for a man and his donuts.

In January 2020, doh'hut found its permanent home at Trevelyan Square, Leeds — a proper bricks-and-mortar hut for hand-rolled, freshly fried donuts made every morning.

But doh'hut was always meant to be more. The shop's destiny was always to become an all things dough destination. And while the donuts took over Trevelyan Square, something else was quietly being born around the corner.

Things in Bread grew out of a love — and a frustration. Tom kept asking the same questions over a supermarket meal deal: why is the bread so dry? Why are the fillings so bland? Why can't anyone give us a better version of the classics we already love? So he spent two years perfecting a bread recipe — a signature shokupan milk loaf: perfectly square, impossibly fluffy, with a crispy crust. Then he took 15 Boar Lane, and brought Leeds the made-to-order sandwich shop it had been missing.

And then came Bake — the bakery that ties it all together, fuelling the whole operation from Lockside. The sausage rolls, the pastries, the things that make mornings worth getting up for.

Three shops. One city. One obsession with doing dough properly.
